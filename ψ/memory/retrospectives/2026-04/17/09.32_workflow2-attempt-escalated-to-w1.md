  sequence (as the 2026-04-17 learning had promised). Instead, the response
  was to tighten the *trigger condition* (PR #189 request_id gate) — which
  reduces the *frequency* of the race but doesn't eliminate it. Worth
  flagging to the user: the word "redesign" in the original ON_HOLD learning
  may turn out to mean "narrower trigger", not "different callback model".
  If so, the ON_HOLD rationale may still hold: a narrower race is still a
  race, and the test assertions can still see the wrong final callback.
- The `services/withdrawalQueue.go` churn in the range is smaller than I
  expected — 4 small commits totalling ~50 LOC. The heavy matcher churn is
  on `services/transactionMatcher.go` (6 commits, ~115 LOC delta).
