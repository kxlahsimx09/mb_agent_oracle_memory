### "No re-evaluation during extraction" discipline — durable lesson

Extraction is restructure, not redesign. The discipline matters because:

1. **Trust in supersede chain integrity** — future readers must be able to assume that supersede-chain endpoints reflect ratification-time decisions; silent re-evaluation during extraction breaks this contract.
2. **Discoverability of design archaeology** — if extraction silently changes wording, comparing pre-extract vs post-extract state requires git archaeology + careful diffing instead of trusting the supersede chain.
3. **Workflow predictability** — extraction passes should be ~25-minute structural mechanics, not 60+ minute redesign sessions.

**If extraction surfaces an ambiguity or design gap → that becomes a NEW pass (4th, 5th) with explicit revisit/amendment shape, not silent change in extraction commit.**
