## Honest Feedback

- The workflow spec assumes W2 is a net-new branch. Real life has long-lived W2 PRs that linger unmerged for a day or two, and new commits land during that window. The "extend the existing PR" path is not in the spec, but it's the right call here. If this pattern persists, add a §Extension sub-procedure to the workflow covering: (1) branch switch + merge main, (2) layer one commit per new in-range commit, (3) update PR body rather than opening a new one, (4) trace chain continuation. Would have saved me 5 minutes of second-guessing.
- The Telegram step is producing good story-led summaries now that the char budget is 700. At 500 I'd have had to drop either the "why baseline didn't bump" context or the "stale-count bank" explanation; losing either makes the message feel like a changelog entry instead of a story. The bump was right.
