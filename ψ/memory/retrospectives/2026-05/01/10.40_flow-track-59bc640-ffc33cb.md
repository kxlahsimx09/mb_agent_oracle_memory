# Honest Feedback

- The §Fast-fix threshold (≤5 flows) is calibrated for fresh drift volume, not pointer-shift volume. A commit that adds a top-of-file constant or a goroutine to a popular controller can mechanically shift dozens of pointers across 9+ flow docs without introducing any semantic drift. The threshold doesn't distinguish "9 flows × 1 mechanical refresh each" from "9 flows × 1 fresh drift each". The retro from 2026-04-30 W9 already noted the same concern; this is the second consecutive overrun within the same week. Suggest spec calibration: split into two thresholds — "≤5 flows with any C/D/E/F drift" (escalate) vs "≤15 flows with only A/B refresh" (proceed). Filing as honest-feedback retro line, not a separate spec edit.
- The `// impl:` annotation prose attached to many pointers carries inline context like "line shifted +1 by the `/checking-count` insertion at line 25" (deposit-slip-upload-admin-approve.md). When I bumped `@e430a06` → `@d2a2738` for `routes/deposit.go:31`, the inline note about the `/checking-count` insertion became confusingly historical (the +1 shift was from `e430a06`, not `d2a2738`). The prose accuracy held but the *origin* of the line drift now needs the reader to read the doc's change log to figure out. Not worth fixing in W9 (would be prose rewriting); flag for next W8 revision.
