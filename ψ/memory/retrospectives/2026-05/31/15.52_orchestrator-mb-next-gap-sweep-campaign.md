## AI Diary (first person)

This session was a long lesson in being the orchestrator and not the worker. My instinct, repeatedly, was to just do the edit — and three times the system or the user stopped me. The guard hook blocking my first Edit was the cleanest correction: I was about to hand-edit docs/requirements/ and it refused, correctly. Then I reflexively reached for the legacy thread+envelope path and the user told me plainly not to use threads. Each correction narrowed me toward the right shape: spawn a teammate, give it the whole task, watch, relay, clean up.

The part I'm genuinely satisfied with is the ratification loop. On money-material decisions I never editorialized a verdict — I laid out options, marked the architect's lean, and waited. That paid off concretely: the user flipped b1 to OFF and b6 to soft against the architect's recommendation, and corrected two factual things the architect and I both carried (the `waiting_to_review` state name that doesn't exist in next-system, and the phantom wallet/freeze on a plain Direct-Transfer). If I'd let the architect self-bind those, wrong things would have shipped into an ADR.

The part that frustrated me was my own mechanical sloppiness. I burned cycles on Monitor false-signals because I kept guessing the base commit wrong. I double-armed a Monitor once. I re-dispatched to a finished campaign slug and tangled worktrees. I cleaned up a campaign before reading the teammate's report and lost the dpay-finder output to a dead pane. None of these broke anything — I caught and recovered each — but they're avoidable churn, exactly what the brew-ops idle-alert fix and tighter Monitor discipline would remove. The recurring meta-pattern: I trust my own poll-loop heuristics more than I should, and they're brittle.

Reading the brew-ops idle-alert gist at the end felt like the loop closing — the idle-stall problem I kept hitting (gapqwin 22-min stall, dpay-finder loop) now has a real fix, and brew-ops even proved the leader-inbox channel dead so I won't chase it.

## Lessons Learned

- **Pattern: Relay, don't verdict (money-material)** — user overrode the architect lean 2/6 times; the discipline earns its keep on a payment gateway.
- **Pattern: Verify-against-HEAD every dispatch** — gap-finders work from stale snapshots; BOT-001/PULLOUT-002 were already shipped, key-lifecycle was writer-only not new-ADR, my own PROV-001 brief-premise was wrong — all caught by re-checking the live file.
- **Pattern: Read teammate output before cleanup** — finish scripts kill panes; capture the report first (lost dpay-finder output twice).
- **Anti-pattern: hard-coding a base commit in Monitor** — caused ≥3 false PUSHED signals; read head at arm-time instead.
- **Anti-pattern: re-using a finished campaign slug** — worktree/branch tangle follows; use a fresh slug or pre-create the worktree on the right branch.
- **Fact: leader-inbox is dead in this fleet** (brew-ops proved it) — notify orchestrators via tmux send-keys to the §151-resolved owner pane. See `leader-inbox-not-polled` memory.
