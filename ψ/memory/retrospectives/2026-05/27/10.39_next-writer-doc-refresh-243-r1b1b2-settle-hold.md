## Honest Feedback

- **The addendum's framing nearly walked me into a P-004 hazard.** "Faithfulness fix, do it now, same PR, needs_response:false" reads as routine doc work — but the actual change contradicted a *ratified* `#decision`. If I had taken the framing at face value and shipped it, PR #261 would have an S2 story silently contradicting its live ADR. The taxonomy that saved it — classify directed fixes as propagate-ratified / grounding-not-contradicting / contradicts-a-ratified-ADR — should be a standing reflex, not a thing I reconstructed mid-task. It's now in memory; future-me should apply it *before* opening the editor on any "correct the story to match production" ask.
- **The orchestrator's own anti-pattern note was instructive.** Its handled_note ("no action envelope — would wake the agent to do nothing") is a good model: a HOLD is closed via handled_note, not a doorbell. I mirrored it (msg 1128 ack, no envelope). Worth remembering that not every thread turn needs an envelope — the envelope is a doorbell for *action*, and an ack of a hold is not action.
- **Trust-label discipline held up well under pressure.** The temptation on B1/B2 was to over-correct (e.g., claim the balance-threshold trigger was removed, or rewrite DTR-002's whole lifecycle). Staying inside "what pg-writer verified + what the brief asked" kept the S2/S4 labels accurate. The discipline that the architect owns the decision and I translate it — not re-derive it — is exactly what kept SETTLE from going off the rails.
