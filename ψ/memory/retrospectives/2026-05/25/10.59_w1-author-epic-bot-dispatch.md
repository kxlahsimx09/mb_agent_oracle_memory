## AI Diary

The useful shape was to treat Bot Dispatch as a control-plane epic, not as a payout sub-story. ADR-8 had enough product-level decisions to justify four stories: choose the bank, control trigger fan-out, recover missed dispatch, and harden bot claim. That split kept the requirement from duplicating PAYOUT-002 while still making the gateway/bankbot boundary explicit.

The current-system Mongo samples were useful for naming and confidence, but not enough to drive S2 by themselves. The S2 trust comes from ADR-8, ADR-4a, ratified flow memory, and the hosted PoC.
