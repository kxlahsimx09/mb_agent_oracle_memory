- **Discrimination-before-dispatch saved a whole next-dev cycle.** The "gateway-side payout claim cooldown" was confidently localized by the tester's EXP1 — yet next-dev then proved it INTENDED (no defect). Had I dispatched next-dev to "fix" it on the tester's framing, it would have chased a non-bug. Verify the premise, let the owning expert adjudicate, attribute the verdict to them (principle 2a held).
- **L3 caught the masking risk the tester itself flagged.** Conservation snapshots keyed on inert cast partner wallets go green trivially; the real money moved through seeded recipients. L3's independent recount on the REAL recipients is what makes the GREEN trustworthy. Runner ≠ grader earned its keep.
- **Idle teammates burn shared account quota → session-limit (user-corrected).** Leaving brew-ops/next-dev/tester idle overnight is the suspected cause of next-investigator hitting the limit. **Close teammates the moment DONE-WHEN is met.**
- **`team-dispatch-finish.sh` does NOT kill the agent process** (helper spawns via its own `tmux new-window`, outside maw's pane manager). Finish merges knowledge + removes the worktree but the idle claude survives. Must ALSO `tmux kill-pane`. Filed as a fix request to brew-ops. Verified: `pgrep -af "claude --agent-id"` after finish still listed the agents.
- **Polling pattern that worked:** background Bash monitor watching the findings-file mtime + pane-idle, re-invoking me on completion — survived the long live runs and the overnight gap. Do NOT grep the agent pane for "BLOCKER:" — the dispatch contract text contains those tokens (false positives); use the findings file as the signal.
