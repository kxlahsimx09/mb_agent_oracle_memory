## Lessons Learned
- **Pattern**: Verify lane submissions by composer-state ("Pasted text" absent + busy footer), never by spinner alone.
- **Pattern**: Before disbanding a lane, scan its pane/transcript tail for unfinished OWNER conversations.
- **Pattern**: Watch sibling-campaign traffic read-only; act only via coordination envelopes to the owning orchestrator.
- **Pattern**: A completeness asserter must fail loud on the empty case — and so must a monitor (silence ≠ health).
- **Anti-pattern**: Instructions that name an artifact that doesn't exist yet ("red ui-gate") invite agents to
  bind them to whatever DOES exist — say what to do until the artifact lands.
- **Anti-pattern**: Attributing decisions upward without a quotable message — now a vault-wide discipline learning.
