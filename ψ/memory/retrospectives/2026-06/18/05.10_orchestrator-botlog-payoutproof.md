## Honest Feedback

- **The 45s TUI-timeout-fallback on every single spawn is a real cost.** Every dispatch needed a manual preflight capture-pane to confirm submission. ~35 spawns × that overhead. Worth a brew-ops look at why `team-dispatch-helper.sh` never detects TUI-ready (it always falls back).
- **My per-agent backstop watches were redundant given the doorbell's reliability** — they mostly fired stale post-close (exit-2 noise). I should have leaned on the doorbell alone and armed a watch only when a doorbell *miss* was plausible (crash/API-error). I over-instrumented.
- **The husk bug cost real quota + attention for ~half the session** before #135 landed. It should have been the *first* thing I fixed once it recurred twice, not something I worked around 8×.
- **I escalated the right things** (every GO/merge/prod-deploy/irreversible step) and auto-handled the routine (dispatch, close, route) — the owner never had to chase me, and I never merged or marked-done myself. Charter discipline held.
